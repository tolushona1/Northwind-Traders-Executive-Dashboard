# Northwind Traders | Executive Dashboard

## Business Question
Using the Northwind Traders dataset, build an executive-level view of the business: How is revenue trending, who are the key customers and products, and how does performance break down by category, shipping company, and time period?

## Tools Used
- Tableau (Public), Northwind Traders dataset

## Approach
1. Followed a guided Tableau tutorial (Kapil Dhiman, Parts 1 & 2) to learn dashboard design principles and Tableau functionality.
2. Built KPI cards for revenue, customer count, order count, average shipping cost, and average discount.
3. Added monthly revenue and quantity trend lines, category and shipping-company breakdowns, and top customer/product views.
4. Added interactive filters for Country, Year, Month, and a "Top N" parameter.
5. Explored the finished dataset independently to pull out additional observations beyond the tutorial.

## Key Insight
The Top Customers view shows a clear top tier: the three highest-revenue customers are closely clustered between £113k–£118k, with a sharp drop-off after that — the 4th-ranked customer generates roughly half as much. This kind of concentration is useful for prioritising account management or flagging churn risk if a top customer were lost.

## Limitations
This dashboard was built by following a tutorial rather than from an original business brief, so the metric choices and layout reflect the tutorial's design rather than an independently scoped analysis — the "What I Noticed" insight above was where I extended past the tutorial itself.
